Quick answers

How do you know if a lens covers a sensor?

Compare the lens image circle with the diagonal of the area you record. If the image circle is equal or larger, it covers. Many lenses cover a bit more than their rated circle with some softening and vignetting in the corners, so test on the camera before the job, especially at close focus and wide open.

How do I get the same framing on another camera?

Multiply the focal length by the ratio between the two sensor widths (or diagonals). A 35 mm on an ALEXA 35 in 4.6K Open Gate gives about the same horizontal view as a 46 mm on an ALEXA Mini LF Open Gate. The tool does this for every lens in the set.

How does anamorphic change the view?

An anamorphic lens squeezes the horizontal view onto the sensor. A 2x lens on a 4:3 area sees as wide as a spherical lens twice as wide would, so the horizontal angle is calculated with the sensor width times the squeeze.

Where does the data come from?

Image circles, focal lengths and mounts from each lens maker's own specifications. Sensor areas from the camera makers' format tables, the same data behind the camera specs pages. Checked 2026-11-08.
