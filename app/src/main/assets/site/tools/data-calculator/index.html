Quick answers

Where do the data rates come from?

From each manufacturer's own specifications, manuals and data rate tables. Every format links to the page it was taken from. When a brand only publishes a rate for one frame rate, the calculator scales it with the frame rate, because RAW and intra-frame codecs store a fixed size per frame.

Why 3 copies?

The usual rule on set: a master and two backups, on different drives, with one of them leaving the set every day. Change it to 2 if production signs off on a single backup.

Why does my drive show less than the calculator says?

Drives are sold in decimal terabytes (1 TB = 1,000 GB) and the calculator uses the same units. Windows shows binary units, so an 8 TB drive appears as about 7.3 TB there. Leave 10 to 20% free on every drive anyway.

How much do I really roll per day?

It depends on the job. As a rough start: commercials and music videos often roll 1 to 3 hours per camera, fiction 2 to 4, documentaries and live events much more. Check with the script supervisor after the first day and adjust.
